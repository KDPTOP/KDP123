term: 35% and 70% royalty options
aka:
  - Ebook royalty rate
definition: The two Kindle ebook royalty rates, paid on the list price excluding VAT. The 70% option needs a
  US list price of $2.99 to $12.99, deducts a delivery cost and applies only to buyers in Amazon's 70%
  territories; other sales earn 35%.
