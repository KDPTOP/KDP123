---
term: Best Sellers Rank (BSR)
aka:
  - Sales Rank
  - Amazon Best Sellers Rank
definition: Amazon's ranking of a book by customer activity relative to other books, shown in the Product
  Details section for up to three categories. It updates at least once a day, weights recent activity more
  heavily, and can move even when your own sales stay the same.
tool: bsr-sales-estimator
see:
  - categories
sources:
  - https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201648140
