---
term: Keywords
aka:
  - KDP keywords
  - Backend keywords
definition: Up to seven words or short phrases you enter for a book so readers can find it on Amazon. They
  must not mention other authors or books, sales rank, promotions such as "free", brands you are not
  authorized to use, or Amazon programs like Kindle+ or KDP Select.
