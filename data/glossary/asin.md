---
term: ASIN
aka:
  - Amazon Standard Identification Number
definition: The unique 10-character code Amazon assigns to every product in its store. Most ASINs start with
  B0, book ASINs can begin with a number, and the ASIN appears in a product URL after /dp/.
see:
  - isbn
sources:
  - https://sell.amazon.com/blog/what-is-an-asin
status: published
verified_at: 2026-11-09
---
