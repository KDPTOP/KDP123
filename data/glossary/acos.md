---
term: ACoS
aka:
  - Advertising cost of sales
  - ROAS
definition: "Ad spend divided by the sales those ads brought in, as a percentage: $50 spent for $100 of sales
  is a 50% ACoS. Amazon Ads says ACoS has to stay below your profit margin for ads to make money; ROAS (return
  on ad spend) is the same ratio turned upside down."
