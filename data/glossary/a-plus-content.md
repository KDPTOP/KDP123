term: A+ Content
definition: Images, text and comparison charts that a KDP author adds to a book's Amazon detail page through
  the A+ Content Manager on the KDP Marketing page. It is created per marketplace, appears within eight
  business days of approval, and cannot include customer reviews, pricing, promotions or links.
