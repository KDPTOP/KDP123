---
term: Low-content book
definition: A print book with minimal or no interior content, usually repetitive pages to fill in, such as
  notebooks, planners, journals and log books; puzzle and coloring books are generally not low-content. It can
  be published without an ISBN but not with a free KDP ISBN, and cannot use Expanded Distribution or series.
