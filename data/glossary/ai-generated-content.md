---
term: AI-generated content
aka:
  - AI-assisted content
definition: Text, images or translations created by an AI tool, which KDP requires you to disclose when you
  publish a book or republish it after edits, even if you edited the output heavily. AI-assisted content,
  which you created yourself and only edited, checked or brainstormed with AI, does not need to be disclosed.
