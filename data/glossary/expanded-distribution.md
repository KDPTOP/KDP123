term: Expanded Distribution
definition: A free KDP option that offers a paperback to US and UK distributors so bookstores, libraries and
  other retailers can order it, paying 40% of the list price minus printing cost. Hardcovers, low-content
  books and some trim sizes, languages and content types cannot use it.
