term: Bleed
definition: Artwork that runs past the trim edge so color reaches the edge of the page after cutting. KDP
  bleed is 0.125 in (3.2 mm) on the top, bottom and outside edges, so a 6 x 9 in page with bleed is 6.125 x
  9.25 in.
