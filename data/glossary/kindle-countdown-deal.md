---
term: Kindle Countdown Deal
aka:
  - KCD
definition: A KDP Select promotion that discounts a Kindle ebook for a limited time on Amazon.com or
  Amazon.co.uk, showing the regular price, the deal price and a countdown clock. You keep your royalty rate,
  so a 70% book still earns 70% below $2.99.
