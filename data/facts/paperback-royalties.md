## Two rates, set by list price

KDP now offers two paperback royalty rates on Amazon marketplaces. The rate depends on the list price in each marketplace:

| Marketplace | 50% at or below | 60% at or above |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Amazon.com | 9.98 USD | 9.99 USD |
| Amazon.co.uk | 7.98 GBP | 7.99 GBP |
| Euro marketplaces | 9.98 EUR | 9.99 EUR |
| Amazon.ca | 13.98 CAD | 13.99 CAD |
| Amazon.com.au | 13.98 AUD | 13.99 AUD |

The printing cost is then subtracted. It depends on page count, ink and paper type, whether the trim is regular or large, and the marketplace where the book was ordered.

## Worked examples (Amazon.com)

Amazon's own examples:

- $15 list price, 333-page regular trim black ink paperback: (0.60 × $15) − $5.00 = $4.00. Through Expanded Distribution: (0.40 × $15) − $5.00 = $1.00.
- 300-page black ink paperback, printing cost $4.60: at $9.99 the royalty is (0.60 × $9.99) − $4.60 = $1.39; at $9.98 it drops to (0.50 × $9.98) − $4.60 = $0.39.

One cent separates the two cases, so pricing just under the threshold is the most common mistake.

## Minimum and maximum prices

KDP will not accept a list price below printing cost divided by the royalty rate, so every sale covers its printing cost. The US maximum is $250 (350 CAD, 250 EUR, 250 GBP, 350 AUD and 30,000 JPY in other marketplaces).

## Reporting and payment

Royalties appear in reports as copies ship; Expanded Distribution units show about 30 days after month end. Amazon-channel royalties are paid about 60 days after the end of the month of sale, Expanded Distribution about 90 days. Paperbacks in Japanese cannot use Expanded Distribution.

Amazon changes these rules; the linked KDP help pages are the authority.
