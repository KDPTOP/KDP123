## What the Tax Profile does

US tax rules require Amazon to collect your tax identity because royalties can count as income. In KDP you complete it under Your Account, then Tax Information, then Complete Tax Profile. The questionnaire generates the right form from your answers: a Form W-8 for non-US persons, or a W-8ECI if your income is connected with a US trade or business. US persons enter a US tax number (SSN, EIN or ITIN) that must match IRS records exactly; KDP's help pages don't walk through the US form by name. Until the status is Complete, you cannot publish new books or update existing ones.

## Withholding for US persons

Once valid information is on file, royalties carry no US tax withholding. Brazil still withholds 15% on Amazon.com.br sales, and Amazon.in sales are subject to 10% Singaporean withholding.

## Withholding for non-US persons

The default US rate is 30% on Amazon.com ebook sales, on print sales from Amazon.com, Amazon.co.jp, Amazon.com.au and Amazon.ca, and on KDP Select Global Fund payments. If your country has an income tax treaty with the US, you may get a lower rate by giving a US TIN or, if you don't have one, your own country's income tax ID.

## Worked example

A non-US author signs a W-8 on September 30, 2026. It stays valid through December 31, 2029. If it isn't renewed by then, any treaty rate reverts to 30%.

## Common mistakes

- Entering a name that differs from the IRS record: SSN names must match the Social Security card, and EIN names the first line of the IRS notice.
- Forgetting that a newly issued TIN can take up to 60 days to reach the IRS database.
- Comparing the 1099 or 1042-S to royalty reports. The forms show payments received, not royalties earned.

Amazon changes these rules; the linked KDP help pages are the authority.
