## Manuscript formats

KDP lists three recommended formats. Word files (DOC/DOCX) usually convert well, though complex formatting may not. KPF files come from Kindle Create, which handles typesetting, themes and the table of contents for you. EPUB files from any third-party tool are accepted if they meet the Kindle Publishing Guidelines, and Amazon suggests validating them in Kindle Previewer before upload. HTML, RTF, plain text and PDF are also accepted, but PDF only in nine Western European languages.

## The end of MOBI

MOBI support ended in two steps: reflowable books on August 1, 2021 and fixed-layout books on March 18, 2025. Use EPUB, DOCX or KPF for reflowable books, and KPF or EPUB for fixed-layout books. Re-uploading as EPUB does not change what readers receive, and the switch has no effect on royalties.

## Cover image

The marketing cover is uploaded separately from the manuscript. Amazon's recommended size is 2,560 pixels high by 1,600 pixels wide (a 1.6:1 ratio), with 2,500 pixels of height for best quality on high-definition screens. Files must be 10,000 pixels or fewer per side and under 50 MB. RGB is the recommended color mode; the newer guidelines say CMYK is converted automatically. Accepted formats differ by page: the cover criteria page lists TIFF and JPEG, while the Kindle Publishing Guidelines add PNG and non-animated GIF and name JPEG as preferred. JPEG works under both.

## Common mistakes

- Stretching a small image to reach 2,560 x 1,600 lowers quality. Amazon says to create a new image instead.
- Adding an HTML cover page inside the book on top of the cover image can show the cover twice or make conversion fail.
- White or very light covers fade into the page; Amazon suggests a 3-4 pixel medium gray border.
- Prices or temporary promotions printed on the cover are not allowed.

Amazon changes these rules; the linked KDP help pages are the authority.
