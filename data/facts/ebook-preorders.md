## How a pre-order works

Setting up a pre-order puts your detail page live with a Pre-order button. You don't need the final manuscript right away; you upload and submit it any time before the deadline. The release date is set in GMT. Public domain ebooks cannot be put on pre-order, and you can have up to 10 active pre-orders at once.

## The 72-hour deadline

The final manuscript must be submitted at least 72 hours before the release date. Book details, the manuscript and the cover can be changed until then. Miss the deadline and the pre-order is canceled, customers are notified and their orders are canceled. After submitting, you cannot make changes for up to 72 hours while the book is reviewed.

## Postponing

The rules changed on September 2, 2026. Pre-orders created on or after that date can be postponed up to 2 times, with the new date no more than 6 months after the original release date. Older pre-orders can be postponed once, by up to 30 days. Exceeding the limit auto-cancels the pre-order, which counts as a cancellation.

## Penalties

KDP keeps an account-level count of canceled pre-orders and missed deadlines. The first is forgiven once. The second removes pre-order privileges for 6 months across your entire catalog: no new pre-orders and no postponing existing ones, though you can still publish without a pre-order. Privileges removed before September 2, 2026 keep the old one-year penalty.

## Worked example

A pre-order created on October 1, 2026 with a release date of March 1, 2027 needs its final file submitted 72 hours earlier, by the start of February 26, 2027 (GMT). It can be pushed back twice, but not past September 1, 2027.

## Common mistakes

- Forgetting to click Submit for pre-order after uploading, which leaves the book in Draft.
- Canceling and recreating a pre-order to reset the date. Each cancellation counts toward the penalty, and the old ASIN cannot be reused.

Amazon changes these rules; the linked KDP help pages are the authority.
