## The formula

Printing cost = fixed cost + (page count × per-page cost). Short books pay only a flat fixed cost; longer books pay a lower fixed cost plus a per-page charge. The cost depends on format, ink (black, standard color, premium color), paper, trim category and the marketplace the reader ordered from. Bleed and cover finish do not change it.

A trim size counts as large if it is more than 6.12 in (155.5 mm) wide or more than 9 in (228.6 mm) tall, and large trims have higher costs.

## Amazon.com paperback costs

| Paperback (US) | Regular trim | Large trim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Black ink, cream/white, 24-110 pages | $2.30 | $2.84 |
| Black ink, cream/white, 110-828 pages | $1.00 + $0.012/page | $1.00 + $0.017/page |
| Black ink, groundwood, 24-112 pages | $2.23 | $2.75 |
| Black ink, groundwood, 114-828 pages | $1.00 + $0.0114/page | $1.00 + $0.0162/page |
| Premium color, 24-40 pages | $3.60 | $4.20 |
| Premium color, 42-828 pages | $1.00 + $0.065/page | $1.00 + $0.08/page |
| Standard color, 72-600 pages | $1.00 + $0.0255/page | $1.00 + $0.0402/page |

## Amazon.com hardcover costs

Black ink hardcovers cost $6.80 flat (large $7.49) at 75 to 108 pages and $5.65 + $0.012 per page (large $0.017) at 110 to 550 pages. Premium color hardcovers cost $5.65 + $0.065 per page (large $0.080) at 75 to 550 pages. Standard color is not offered for hardcover.

## Worked example

A 300-page black ink 6 x 9 in paperback sold on Amazon.com: $1.00 + (300 × $0.012) = $4.60. The same book in premium color: $1.00 + (300 × $0.065) = $20.50.

## Common mistakes

- Assuming trim size never matters. Only the regular/large category does, but crossing it (6.14 x 9.21 in is large) raises the per-page cost by over 40% for black ink.
- Choosing color ink for a book with a few color images. Every page is billed at the color rate.

Amazon changes these rules; the linked KDP help pages are the authority.
