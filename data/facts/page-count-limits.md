## Paperback limits (kdp.amazon.com)

The minimum is 24 pages for every option except standard color, which starts at 72. Maximums depend on paper, ink and trim size:

| Trim size | White | Cream | Groundwood | Standard color | Premium color |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 x 8 in through 8 x 10 in (11 sizes) | 828 | 776 | 812 | 600 | 828 |
| 8.25 x 6 in, 8.25 x 8.25 in | 800 | 750 | 784 | 600 | 800 |
| 8.27 x 11.69 in | 780 | 730 | 764 | Not available | 590 |
| 8.5 x 8.5 in, 8.5 x 11 in | 590 | 550 | 578 | 600 | 590 |

The first row covers 5 x 8, 5.06 x 7.81, 5.25 x 8, 5.5 x 8.5, 6 x 9, 6.14 x 9.21, 6.69 x 9.61, 7 x 10, 7.44 x 9.69, 7.5 x 9.25 and 8 x 10 in. The white, cream and groundwood columns are black ink.

## Hardcover limits

Every hardcover trim size (5.5 x 8.5, 6 x 9, 6.14 x 9.21, 7 x 10 and 8.25 x 11 in) accepts 75 to 550 pages in black ink on white or cream paper and in premium color. Standard color is not available for hardcovers.

## Thresholds worth knowing

- 79 pages: the minimum for spine text on a paperback cover (80 in Cover Creator).
- 110 pages: below this, a black ink paperback on Amazon.com pays only the flat fixed printing cost.
- 150, 300, 500 and 700 pages: the points where the required inside margin increases.

## Common mistakes

- Planning a thick book on cream paper. Cream has the lowest cap at 776 pages on standard sizes.
- Choosing standard color for a short picture book. It needs 72 pages; premium color starts at 24.
- Moving a paperback over 550 pages to hardcover. It must be cut down or split.

Amazon changes these rules; the linked KDP help pages are the authority.
