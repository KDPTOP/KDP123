## What KENP measures

Amazon calculates a book's KENP with standard font, line height and spacing, so page counts are the same across devices and settings. Counting starts at the Start Reading Location, which Amazon usually sets at chapter 1, and runs to the end of the book. Images, charts and graphs count toward KENP.

## How the per-page rate is set

Amazon reviews the size of the KDP Select Global Fund each month and allocates part of it to each marketplace. The KENP rate for a marketplace is that marketplace's fund divided by the total KENP read there that month. Your earnings are your pages read (and, since the Kindle+ change, pages listened to) multiplied by that rate. The fund for one month is announced near the 15th of the next; for example, the September fund comes out around October 15. Allocation depends on exchange rates, reading behavior and local subscription prices, so rates vary by marketplace.

## Worked example

A subscriber borrows a 400-KENP novel, reads pages 1 to 250, then listens to pages 1 to 400 in the audiobook. You are paid for 400 KENP: 250 read plus the 150 new pages heard. Pages 1 to 250 heard a second time are treated as re-reading. Even a 5,000-KENP book pays no more than 3,000 KENP per customer.

## Reporting and payment

The KENP report shows Kindle+ Read and Kindle+ Listened pages by marketplace and is finalized near the 15th of the following month. Kindle+ earnings are paid with your other KDP royalties on the same schedule.

## Common mistakes

- Treating a past KENP rate as fixed. It is recalculated every month.
- Paying or rewarding readers for reading. Amazon prohibits incentives of any kind for Kindle+ reads, including gift cards, bonus content and contest entries.
- Assuming reads stop when you leave Select. Subscribers who already borrowed the book can keep reading, and those pages still earn from the fund.

Amazon changes these rules; the linked KDP help pages are the authority.
