## How it works

A+ Content adds images, text and comparison tables to your book's detail page, in the From the Publisher section that most readers reach by scrolling. Go to the KDP Marketing page, choose a marketplace in the A+ Content section and open Manage A+ Content. Name the project, set its language, add and arrange modules, then apply the ASINs of your books and submit for approval.

## Marketplaces

From kdp.amazon.com you can publish A+ Content to Amazon.com, .ca, .com.au, .com.br, .com.mx, .co.uk, .de, .es, .fr, .in, .it and .nl. Content from kdp.amazon.co.jp can only go to Amazon.co.jp. Shoppers see content in their preferred language, so Spanish A+ Content on Amazon.com only shows to shoppers who chose Spanish.

## Image rules

The guidelines require JPG or PNG files in RGB (CMYK is not supported), each under 2 MB, at 72 dpi or more, and ban animated images. The creation guide recommends 300 DPI for best results and treats anything under 200 DPI as low resolution. Images must be sharp, without watermarks or text too small to read on a phone, and different from your existing image gallery.

## Content restrictions

A+ Content cannot include pricing or promotional language ("free", "buy now"), time-sensitive words ("new", "latest") or holidays, references to Kindle+, customer reviews, more than four quotes, web links, contact details, guarantees, or comparisons with competitors. Awards older than 2 years are not allowed, and claims like "#1 rated" are banned.

## Worked example

A cookbook author can make one project with a banner image, two recipe spreads and a comparison chart of her three cookbooks, apply both the ebook and paperback ASINs, and publish it on Amazon.com. It goes live within eight business days if compliant.

## Common mistakes

- Pasting a reader review as a pull quote. Only publications or public figures are allowed.
- Reusing the cover or gallery images rather than unique visuals.

Amazon changes these rules; the linked KDP help pages are the authority.
